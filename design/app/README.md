# App design: handoff

The design is law. Where a ticket or a later document disagrees with the mocks, the mocks win. The exceptions are in this file only.

## What to read, in this order

1. `docs/requirements.md`, section 0 only. Sections 1 to 15 are the backlog. Do not build them.
2. `public/designs/12/index.html`: design 12, "Flyer". It fixes the home page and the frame of every module. Live copy: https://travel-ca8f9.web.app/designs/12/
3. `public/designs/20/index.html`: design 20, "The page moves forward". It fixes what is inside a module: the date options, the stay cards, the party list, the sheets and the map card. Live copy: https://travel-ca8f9.web.app/designs/20/
4. `docs/trip-app/plan.md`: the domain model, the data contract, the edge cases and the ticket order.

All screens of design 12 sit on line 166 of its file; all screens of design 20 sit on line 183 of its file. Tickets name a screen as "12/3" or "20/5".

## Exceptions to the mocks

1. **Stays and photos are examples.** The stays in the mocks are made up, and their photos are scenery. The app shows what friends add.
2. **The crew is an example.** The initials JD, TO, KA, MB, DE, SN, RJ, LC and RB are sample friends.
3. **Modules are equals.** Dates, Stay, Parties, Links and Map are module buttons of equal weight, in a fixed order that means nothing. One module is open at a time; opening one closes the other; all start closed. Stay exists only after the organizer confirms the dates. A settled module shows its result on its button ("13–23 Feb · confirmed", "Cliffside villa · confirmed"). Links is a module the mocks do not draw, built from the Add a place sheet's pattern. When the dates and every stay are confirmed, a small line under the title reads "All set". (Owner, 23 Nov 2026.)
4. **A value the link reader cannot read becomes an input.** On screen 20/5 the name, the area and "sleeps" come from the link. When the reader fails or a value is missing, the same position holds an input for that value.
5. **No Directions button.** The owner removed it on 23 Nov 2026. Do not add it back.
6. **Only the title block and the cover follow the hype style.** (Owner, 24 Nov 2026; this replaces "the game-cover style stays on the hype pages".) The flyer's title block is the hype page's: the trip's name ("Bali · Koh Phangan") in Anton with a white fill, a black outline, a hard shadow and a slight tilt, on one line at 375 px; under it, in the pink Yellowtail script with a dark outline so it reads on the light page, "(Indonesia, Thailand) · Feb 2027" (two lines when one does not fit). The month is the anchor's month and year, fixed; it is not the season's span (owner, 24 Nov 2026). It replaces the "February 2027 · Full Moon Party 22 Feb" line; the Full Moon Party is not highlighted under the title. Screen readers hear "Bali and Koh Phangan, Indonesia and Thailand, February 2027". The cover (invitation and home page, phone and desktop) is hype option 5's collage, drawn by `public/hype/cover/` in a frame over the cliff photo: tilted photo cards with white borders, no title, no button, no sound, at most two muted clips; under reduced motion or Save-Data it is the cliff photo alone. Everything else keeps the mocks' clean layout: module names, sheets and body text do not change; the colours of buttons and cards follow exception 19.

8. **The organizer votes too, and may confirm any option.** Screen 20/3 shows the organizer without Yes / Maybe / No and with "Confirm these dates" on the most-yes option only. The organizer is a friend too, so the buttons stay; every option has "Confirm these dates", the most-yes one as the main button and the others in the second style, because an option nobody said yes to may be confirmed (plan, case 12). "Organizer view." sits inside the modules where the organizer acts, as on 20/3, not above the home page.
9. **Phone and desktop.** The mocks draw a phone only. On a phone the app is the mocks' one column, and once a friend has joined, the cover photo is shorter (at most 240 px) so the first module buttons show without scrolling; the invitation keeps its tall photo. Opening a module scrolls it to the top of the window. From 900 px up the page is centred, at most 1200 px wide, in two columns: on the left, sticky, the flyer (photo, organizer's avatar, trip name, date line, "6 of 8 replied", "All set"); on the right, the module buttons and the open module. The invitation puts the photo on the left and the trip, the note and the replies on the right. A sheet becomes a centred dialog. The Map is taller. Type and spacing scale with the window, from 16 px at 900 px to 19 px at about 1900 px. At most two columns; the owner's limit is three. (Owner, 23 Nov 2026.)
10. **Stay cards are Airbnb cards.** Screen 20/4 draws a stay as a row with a small photo. The app draws it as a card: a large photo with rounded corners, the heart and its count over the photo's top right corner, then the name, one line of facts, "Open listing" with who hearted it, and, for the organizer, "Confirm this stay" across the card. One card per row on a phone; two per row in the desktop column. Words and order are 20/4's. (Owner, 23 Nov 2026.)
11. **Friends add their own dates.** Screen 20/2 lists the organizer's options only. Under the options sits "Add dates" (the Links module's second-style button); it opens the shared sheet with "From" and "To" and "Add". The new option shows for everyone at once, sorted by its first day, with "· added by KA" ("added by you" for your own) on its count line, in 20/4's words, and the adder's Yes. An option need not include 22 Feb, and no option marks it: 20/2's line "Each includes the Full Moon Party, Mon 22 Feb." is gone, and options are plain ranges with their counts (owner: "Stop highlighting the Full Moon Party."). The adder, or an organizer, sees "Remove" on an option while nobody else has voted on it. (Owner, 24 Nov 2026: "there should be an option to create your own date interval, and that interval then shows for everyone else.")

12. **The title names the anchor's month.** The mocks write "February 2027". The trip runs into March, but the title block shows the anchor's month only, "Feb 2027" (exception 6); the season's span lives in the dates options. (Owner, 24 Nov 2026.)

13. **The map looks like a modern travel map.** The mock loads Leaflet with OpenStreetMap's own tiles. The app draws OpenFreeMap's Positron vector map with MapLibre, in the app's colours: warm land, soft teal water, muted roads, one English name per label. Pins are white pills with the kind's icon on the accent's soft tint and the name; the open pin fills with the accent; a count pin ("3 pins") shows its icon on the accent. The camera flies to a destination and to an opened pin, and jumps when the device asks for reduced motion. Attribution stays visible and small. Words, pills, grouping, the card and the list fallback are unchanged. (Owner, 23 Nov 2026: "The map could look sleeker".)

14. **Add to map.** The mocks have no way for a friend to put a place on the map. Under the map, "Add to map" opens a search ("Search for a place", suggestions from Photon as the friend types, kept inside the destination); a pick drops a crosshair pin and fills the name; the pin drags; "Can't find it? Tap the map instead" falls back to a tap. Under the map: Name, "What is it?" (Event, Food, Beach, Activity, Other), "Link (optional)", "Add", and "Cancel". The pin shows for everyone at once with its kind's icon; its card says "added by KA", holds the link, and shows "Remove" to the friend who added it and to an organizer. A friend's place cannot be edited. (Owner, 24 Nov 2026: "I'm confused how to add something to the map.")

15. **Show details sheet.** A tap on a show (anywhere but "I'm in", "Edit" and the link button) opens a sheet the mocks do not draw, in the shared Sheet: who is playing in full, its one plain line, the date, "Savaya · Uluwatu, Bali" with the venue's line, who is in with names, "I'm in", then "See on the map" (the Map opens on that venue's destination, centred on it, its card open) and "Event page" (the event's own page, in a new tab). No Directions button and no link out to a maps app (exception 5). Last-year lines open no sheet. (Owner, 24 Nov 2026.)
16. **Parties are called Events.** On screen the module, its button, its sheets and its state line say "Events" and "event"; "Who's playing" and the name "Full Moon Party" stay. Code keeps its words (`parties`, `show`). (Owner, 24 Nov 2026.)
17. **Every venue shows.** Screen 20/7 lists only venues with shows. The app lists every venue the trip knows for each destination: venues with a show this year first, by their first night, then the rest by name, each with its one plain line under the name. A venue with nothing this year says "Nothing announced yet." and, under "Last season:", up to three of last year's acts there, newest first. Each event carries a one-line "about" under its name (one line, cut with "…" on a phone) and, when it has an https page, a small link button before "I'm in". A weekly show (a party every Sunday, say) shows once under its venue with "Every / Sun" in place of the date tile, before the dated shows. (Owner, 24 Nov 2026.) This is the list under "All"; under "Big nights", the default, only the big venues and friends' own events show, and the map draws the big venues only (plan, D18).

18. **Faces and photos.** (Owner, 24 Nov 2026.) A friend may add a photo when joining; every circle that shows initials shows the photo instead when there is one. Once joined, your own face sits beside the organizer's on the flyer, small; tapping it opens "Your photo". On the organizer's own phone the organizer's circle is that button. A saved "I'm in" drops one short burst of confetti (none for Maybe or Can't, none under reduced motion).

19. **The app uses the hype colours.** (Owner, 24 Nov 2026: "I said the app should be GTA colour, so at minimum the buttons should change.") The mocks' green accent becomes black (#111) with white text (owner, 24 Nov 2026; yellow stays on the splash). Every main button and every selected one (a reply, Yes / Maybe / No, a chip, "I'm in" on an event, a hearted stay, the "Most yes" badge) is black with a 2.5 px black outline, and a light edge where it sits on the charcoal page; other buttons are white with the same outline. Everything is flat: no raised or offset shadows on buttons, cards, chips or sheets (owner, 24 Nov 2026); only the title's lettering keeps the hype title's outline and shadow. Module cards carry the black outline. The page behind the cards is charcoal #23211f (owner, 24 Nov 2026), and text directly on it (the replied line, the note, "Been here before? Tap your name:", "Organizer sign-in") is light; cards, sheets and buttons stay white or yellow. Links and small accents inside cards are black; pink #ff3cac is used only as a colour. Body text, layout and spacing stay as the mocks draw them. The tokens live in `web/src/app/tokens.css`; the page colour and its text colours are one place there.

20. **The organizer edits the invite title.** (Owner, 24 Nov 2026: link previews read like an invitation, and "the organizer can change the title".) A shared link's preview reads "You're invited to “Indonesia/Thailand Trip 2027”. Tap here to access"; the name in the quotes is the invite title, "Indonesia/Thailand Trip 2027" until an organizer changes it. For an organizer only, the foot of the page holds the quiet link "Edit invite" where a friend sees "Organizer sign-in". It opens the shared sheet "Invite title": one field with the title (at most 60 characters), "Link preview" over a small grey link bubble (the preview picture, the line "You’re invited to “…”. Tap here to access" as the organizer types, the site's name), "Shared links show this after the next deploy.", and "Save". A blank title is refused with "Type a title."; a refused write says "This was not saved.", a failed one "No connection". The flyer's title block does not change. The page files carry the title from the next deploy (`scripts/stamp-preview.mjs`), because Messages and WhatsApp read the files, not the app.

21. **The organizer makes the links.** (Owner, 23 Nov 2026: "I should be able to generate an invite link, instead of asking you.") For an organizer only, the foot of the page holds the quiet link "Invite friends" before "Edit invite". It opens the shared sheet "Invite friends": the crew link in full in a grey box, "Copy link" (it reads "Copied" for 2 seconds; where the clipboard refuses, the link's text is selected instead) and "Share" (the phone's share sheet, with the invite title as its text; shown only where the browser has one). Under a hairline, "Add an organizer", the line "Anyone with this link can confirm dates and stays.", and "Make an organizer link", which saves a new organizer key and shows its link in the same box with its own "Copy link" and "Share". The sheet never shows a key made before; each opening starts from "Make an organizer link". A refused write says "This was not saved.", a failed one "No connection". Friends never see "Invite friends".

22. **Trip texts.** (Owner, 23 Nov 2026: friends may add a phone number, optional; the organizer can text the crew.) The photo sheet is now titled "You". It and the join sheet (after the photo) hold "Phone for trip texts (optional)": a country picker ("+1" by default) and a phone field, with "Only trip updates from the organizer. Reply STOP to stop." under it; "You" adds "Save number", "Change number" and "Remove number". Friends never see each other's numbers. At the foot of the page, beside "Edit invite", an organizer has "Text the crew": a sheet with the message (300 characters, with a counter), "Who gets it" (Everyone with a number, I'm in, Maybe), the text as it arrives ("Bali · Koh Phangan trip: <message> <short trip link>"), how many it reaches, and Send; a confirm step names the count and the most it can cost (count × $0.15); then each person shows "Sent", "Not sent · <reason>" or "Not confirmed", with "Send again to failed". At most 30 people per text and 5 texts a day per trip.
