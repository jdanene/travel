# Hype page brief

The owner's intent for the splash page, collected 23 Nov 2026. Every hype design follows it.

1. **Job:** get the crew hyped for the trip. The page asks nothing. No buttons to choose, no forms.
2. **Look:** old-school crime-game style that catches the eye: inked panels, heavy condensed type, loading screens, "mission passed" moments.
3. **Media:** real photos and videos only. Nothing fake: no cartoons, no drawn people, no AI images. Lots of pictures and clips. The crew's own trip media first; licensed stock photos of hot club nights are welcome.
4. **Vibe:** hot clubs and hot women (the group prefers Latina-looking women), tables, beaches, adrenaline.
5. **No ads:** no artist names, venues, lineups, dates, tickers or tips.
6. **No filler words:** no captions such as "last trip" or "night party". The only words are TRAVEL, the destinations "Bali · Koh Phangan", and the gag "WASTED / Drink water this time" over the photo of a friend on a drip. The WASTED photo cycles with the other pictures and shows whole, so the drip is visible. It is funny, but never the highlight: no fixed WASTED panel. It sits at the bottom of the page: on a wall it opens in the last (bottom) tile and only ever returns there, once per round of the pictures; in a slideshow or a strip it is last. Never the first panel, never a full-screen opening.
7. **Sound:** keep the music in the clips. Browsers start videos muted, so a small sound button turns it on.
8. **Destinations:** Bali (Indonesia) and Koh Phangan (Thailand). Not Singapore.
9. **The winners:** options 1 and 13. The main link (travel-ca8f9.web.app) opens one of the two at random. Each gets one button that opens the app (the only button on a hype page). The other options stay at /hype/.
10. **Privacy:** pages are `noindex`. No GPS in any published file. No racy footage of identifiable people from private parties.
11. **When:** February and March 2027. The Full Moon Party is on 22 February. No December or January.
12. **Pace:** a photo stays about 2.5 s full screen and 4 s on a panel, with a soft fade. A clip plays to its end before it switches. (7 to 15 s felt stuck; 1.3 to 2 s felt too quick.)
13. **Every option** includes every clip, including the party clip, and every place photo. The party clip comes early.
